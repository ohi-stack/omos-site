## Canonical definition

OneGodian Particle Science™ is the founder-defined theoretical and research discipline within OneGodian Science concerned with proposed fundamental particles, fields, interactions, coherence, and the measurable structure of physical reality.

The framework must distinguish clearly between:

1. **Established physics** — experimentally supported particles and interactions recognized in contemporary physics.
2. **OneGodian theoretical models** — founder-defined hypotheses or mathematical/conceptual extensions proposed for formalization and testing.
3. **Metaphysical interpretation** — philosophical meaning assigned to unity, source, consciousness, coherence, or reality that is not represented as experimentally established particle physics.

## Historical particle framework

The earlier OneGodian particle framework includes four named candidate theoretical entities:

- **O-Particuli™** — foundational/source-level particle concept.
- **Q-Fluxon™** — flux, transition, energetic, or state-change concept.
- **S-Infinitron™** — continuity, scale, persistence, or infinity-oriented concept.
- **O-Harmoniq™** — coherence, harmonization, or interaction-balancing concept.

These names are preserved as historical/theoretical constructs. They are not represented as experimentally discovered elementary particles unless future reproducible evidence establishes otherwise.

- canonical name and symbol;
- formal definition;
- proposed properties;
- mathematical representation;
- interaction rules;
- predicted observable;
- experimental method;

## Public-safe research rule

No OneGodian particle hypothesis should be presented as established particle physics solely because it is internally coherent, symbolically meaningful, mathematically expressible, or agreed upon by multiple AI systems. Scientific status advances only through defined models, reproducible calculations, testable predictions, evidence, and independent verification.

## OMOS relationship

OMOS may store, classify, compare, simulate, and version OneGodian Particle Science™ records, but OMOS model agreement or alignment scoring does not constitute physical verification. Experimental evidence and scientific validation remain separate from governed synthesis.
