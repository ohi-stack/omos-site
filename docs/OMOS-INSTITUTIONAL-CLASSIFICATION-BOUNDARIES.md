# OMOS Institutional Classification Boundaries

**Status:** Public-Safe / Compliance-Oriented Guidance

## Purpose

This document preserves the separation between OneGodian identity/framework language, ONEGODIAN, LLC commercial activity, and Indigenous Nation of Onegodia (INO) governance/religious-society functions.

It is intended to reduce misclassification in public, banking, payment-processing, software, and institutional contexts.

## Core Separation

### ONEGODIAN, LLC
Use for:

- commercial software;
- OMOS products and services;
- intellectual property;
- publishing;
- education products;
- digital infrastructure;
- developer tools;
- commerce;
- consulting and implementation.

ONEGODIAN, LLC should not be described as a governmental or sovereign authority.

### Indigenous Nation of Onegodia (INO)
Use only where appropriate for its separately documented internal governance, religious-society, community, and membership functions.

INO terminology should not be imported into LLC product claims merely for branding effect.

## Public OMOS Positioning

OMOS should be presented as a founder-authored operating, alignment, orchestration, documentation, and decision-support framework/platform.

Public descriptions should distinguish:

- implemented runtime capability;
- specification;
- prototype;
- internal belief/identity language;
- commercial software service;
- governance/religious-society material.

## Identity Boundary

OneGodian identity and Belief Mapper outputs are voluntary internal identity/educational constructs. They do not by themselves create:

- government-issued identity;
- citizenship under federal/state law;
- immunity from law;
- authority over non-participants;
- tax-exempt status;
- banking privileges;
- legal jurisdiction over outside parties.

## Timekeeping Boundary

OTS-V5 may be displayed as a supplemental internal timekeeping system. UTC/Gregorian remain controlling for system-of-record, civil, legal, financial, banking, tax, and government-facing records.

## Alignment Boundary

OMOS alignment scores are internal decision-support metrics. They must not be represented as objective scientific measurements of:

- morality;
- spirituality;
- divine favor;
- human worth;
- legal entitlement;
- medical or psychological status.

## Council Boundary

Multi-model consensus is not proof of truth. OMOS must preserve the distinction among:

- model convergence;
- evidence support;
- factual verification;
- interpretive alignment;
- unresolved uncertainty;
- human approval.

## Financial / Commerce Boundary

OMOS commercial products should be sold as software, education, digital tools, subscriptions, implementation, or related services consistent with the actual deliverables.

Do not represent ordinary digital-product sales as:

- deposits;
- bank accounts;
- securities;
- investment returns;
- guaranteed yield;
- tax-exempt contributions;
- government-backed instruments;

unless a separately reviewed legal structure expressly supports that classification.

## Banking / Processor Description

A concise commercial description may state:

> ONEGODIAN, LLC develops and provides software, digital infrastructure, educational content, publishing, and related technology services, including OMOS decision-support and orchestration tools.

Where OneGodian identity content appears inside an LLC product, describe it as authored framework/IP/educational content unless the context specifically and lawfully concerns INO religious-society activity.

## Claims Rule

Public claims should be scoped by version and evidence. Avoid categorical statements such as "fully compliant," "legally recognized everywhere," "verified truth," or "production-ready" unless the specific claim has documented support.

## Repository Rule

Any document containing stronger governance, spiritual, religious, legal, or sovereignty language should identify its context and entity boundary. The repository should prefer precise labels over symbolic ambiguity.
