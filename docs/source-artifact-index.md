- **ODIN / QR-V / OBP-1** — identifiers, registry, verification, and provenance within their documented scopes.
- **Humans** — final authority for approvals and decisions where required.

## Status Vocabulary

- **Active** — implemented, documented, testable, and available in the current runtime.
- **Functional** — working in source/runtime within a stated scope; deployment/production evidence may be separate.
- **Available** — usable but dependent on configuration or another authoritative service.
- **Planned** — accepted into roadmap, not operational.
- **Draft** — documentation/specification exists but production verification is incomplete.
- **Archived** — retained for history, not current behavior.
