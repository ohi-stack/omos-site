Connections • Adapters • Provider-Neutral Integration

Connect external systems without rewriting OMOS core logic.

The OMOS Connection & Adaptation Layer separates access from translation. A Connector establishes authenticated access to an external platform. An Adapter translates that platform's native objects and actions into a stable OMOS contract.

Four connection classes

Model

OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, xAI, OLLM, and future model providers.

Data

GitHub, Drive, Notion, databases, WordPress, registries, and approved knowledge systems.

Action

Authorized GitHub, WordPress, commerce, email, calendar, deployment, and workflow operations.

Environment

Browser/computer-use runtimes, local tools, 3D environments, devices, robotics, IoT, and future interfaces.

Stable interface

connect() · authenticate() · capabilities() · health() · read() · search() · invoke() · write() · subscribe() · cancel() · disconnect()

The external mechanism may be an API, SDK, MCP server, browser/computer-use interface, CLI, webhook, local process, or agent. OMOS should govern authorization, provenance, policy, and verification independently of that mechanism.

Connection Registry

Each connection should identify platform, adapter, connection class, protocol, authentication method, capabilities, permissions, Human Gate requirements, environment, health, version, last successful operation, rate limits, audit policy, and verification policy.

Security boundary

Connection does not equal authorization. A model or tool may expose a capability without being permitted to use it. Credentials remain server-side, least privilege applies, and consequential writes or external execution may require separate approval.
