Service, Evidence & Human-Authority Boundary

OMOS provides software-assisted analysis and decision support. Outputs can contain errors, incomplete information, or unsupported model claims. Model agreement is not factual verification. Evidence status, provider availability, persistence status, and maturity must remain visible. Human authorization remains controlling for consequential decisions. OMOS does not replace qualified legal, financial, medical, tax, or other professional advice.
