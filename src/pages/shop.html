Why the Catalog Is Structured This Way

General AI access is increasingly common. OMOS is intended to earn payment by solving the harder operational problems around AI use: clarifying messy inputs, comparing conflicting outputs, preserving meaningful dissent, showing uncertainty, maintaining human authorization, and creating reviewable Decision Records.

A future Developer/API plan should be introduced only after authentication, metering, rate limits, billing enforcement, API stability, and support commitments are operational and documented.

Service & Verification Boundary

OMOS provides software-assisted analysis, comparison, governance, and decision support. Outputs may contain errors or incomplete information. Multi-model agreement is comparative evidence, not independent factual verification. Customers remain responsible for consequential decisions and for obtaining qualified professional advice where required. Availability of specific AI providers, connectors, persistent storage, subscriptions, and usage allowances depends on the capabilities actually enabled and verified in the current OMOS production runtime.
