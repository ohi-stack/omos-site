How classification works

The mapper uses only self-reported answers. Formal OneGodian and Elder results require explicit self-identification; theological similarity alone is not enough. OneGodian Ally remains a separate opt-in relationship/status rather than a required journey stage.

Privacy and authority boundary

Belief responses can be sensitive personal information. This first browser experience does not save them. The result is educational and reflective; it is not a governmental, legal, clinical, scientific, employment, housing, credit, healthcare, or objective spiritual classification.
