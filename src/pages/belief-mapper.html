Belief Mapper • Identity Reflection • Product Specification

Discover where your existing beliefs intersect with the OneGodian framework.

The Belief Mapper is designed as an explanatory reflection tool. It maps a person's stated beliefs across seven dimensions and, when the runtime result engine is complete, may present a journey-stage profile with an explanation of what influenced the result. It is not intended to convert users, determine legal status, or declare a person's religious identity for them.

Current maturity: Specification / product defined. The complete runtime, canonical weighting/scoring rules, privacy validation, manifest/API/browser parity, and end-to-end result testing remain required before this experience should be represented as a completed live classifier.

Seven belief dimensions

Ontology

What do you believe about the nature of ultimate reality?

Unity

Do you believe the source of all existence is singular or multiple?

Relationship

How do you understand your personal relationship to that source?

Tradition

What tradition, if any, informs your current belief?

Identity

How do you currently identify your spiritual belief?

Community

What role does community play in your belief practice?

Purpose

Do you believe your life has a purpose given by a higher source?

Journey-stage model

Seeker

Exploring the concept of One God; no formal OneGodian identity claimed.

Believer

Affirms belief in One God as primary truth and is considering the OneGodian identity.

OneGodian

Formally self-identifies as OneGodian and participates in the ecosystem.

Elder

A long-term OneGodian who contributes knowledge, guidance, and community leadership.

OneGodian Ally

OneGodian Ally is a separate relationship/status for a supporter, friend, learner, or respectful observer. It should not be forced into the four-stage journey progression.

Result design requirement

A future result should show the seven-dimensional belief profile, the journey-stage result where applicable, and a plain-language explanation of the responses that influenced it. OMOS should not present an unexplained score as objective truth, and no canonical weights should be invented unless they are separately defined, versioned, and approved.

Privacy and user control

Belief responses are sensitive personal information. The production implementation should minimize collection, explain retention and use, require appropriate consent for persistence, and avoid exposing responses to unrelated connectors or providers. Users should be able to understand what is being evaluated before submitting.
