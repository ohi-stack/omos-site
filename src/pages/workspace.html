Workspace • Ask → Record → History

Turn a difficult question into a reviewable decision.

The OMOS workspace is the customer-facing operating surface. It is designed to show what the system did at each stage instead of hiding the reasoning process behind a single answer.

01 INPUT

Ask OMOS

State the decision, question, document, or options you want reviewed.

02 LAYER 1

Distill

Extract objective, facts, claims, requirements, constraints, contradictions, missing information, and risk without material distortion.

03 ALIGN

Alignment

Evaluate dimension-level fit, hard gates, evidence support, verifiability, and execution readiness.

04 COUNCIL

Compare

Collect independent model outputs, then cross-review without self-review. Provider failures should produce a visible degraded state rather than hidden substitution.

05 SYNTHESIZE

Governed Synthesis

Separate conclusions, evidence status, uncertainty, dissent, alternatives, recommendation, and next actions.

06 REVIEW

Human Gate

Approve or reject the disposition where human authorization is required. Approval records authority; it does not turn a claim into truth.

07 RECORD

Decision Record

Retain timestamps, versions, hashes, provider provenance, stage state, synthesis, human disposition, and verification state where persistence is enabled.

HISTORY

Reopen

Search and revisit governed runs. Restart-safe history remains production-verified only after OMOS-REF-0001 passes.

Decision result contract

A strong OMOS decision result should include: Decision Summary; what is known; what is not known; assumptions; options; risks; AI agreement/disagreement where Council is used; recommended path; conditions that would change the recommendation; confidence/evidence state; human-review requirement; and a Decision Record where persistence is available.
