Operational Workspace

Ask. Review. Decide. Reopen.

OMOS Workspace is the operating surface for questions, decisions, documents, Council runs, Human Gate review, Decision Records and history.

Start Ask OMOSOpen History

1 · Ask

Submit a supported question, decision, document or project requirement.

2 · Distill

Layer 1 extracts objective, facts, claims, constraints, contradictions, missing information and quarantined instructions.

3 · Align

Evaluate explicit dimensions and hard gates without treating an opaque composite score as truth.

4 · Council

Compare available independent model outputs, cross-review, provenance and disagreement.

5 · Synthesize

Produce governed common ground, material remainders, uncertainty, evidence needs and recommendation.

6 · Human Gate

An authorized person approves or rejects the governed result before final disposition.

7 · Record & History

Persist and reopen the Decision Record where durable production persistence is enabled.

Operational boundary

Model agreement is not factual verification. Consequential external actions require separate authorization through the appropriate execution system.
