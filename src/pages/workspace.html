Run modes

Simulation uses simulated provider responses. Hybrid combines live and simulated providers. Live means all requested provider calls were live. Degraded means a requested provider or required capability failed or fell back. Provider availability does not establish factual truth.

Operating boundary

OMOS is decision-support and governed intelligence infrastructure. Model agreement is comparative evidence, not factual verification. Consequential legal, financial, identity, credential, registry, infrastructure, or external execution remains subject to human authorization and applicable professional review.
