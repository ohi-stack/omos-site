The problem OMOS solves

AI can answer. OMOS helps you decide.

Conflicting answers

Compare independent model outputs and see agreement, contradiction, missing evidence, and supported dissent instead of choosing an answer by instinct.

Messy information

Distill a question, document, project, or choice into objectives, facts, claims, requirements, risks, unknowns, and decision conditions.

Hidden uncertainty

Keep uncertainty visible. OMOS separates model convergence from factual verification and makes degraded or unavailable providers explicit.

No decision trail

Preserve the governed result, provider provenance, human disposition, version information, and record lineage where persistence is enabled.

One governed workflow

From question to reviewable record.

INPUT — Ask OMOS: define the decision, question, document, or project.
LAYER 1 — Distill: extract the objective, evidence, claims, constraints, contradictions, missing information, and risk.
ALIGN — Evaluate: apply explicit alignment dimensions, hard gates, evidence support, and execution-readiness checks.
COUNCIL — Compare: collect independent outputs from configured providers and cross-review them without self-review.
SYNTHESIZE — Govern: preserve agreement, dissent, evidence gaps, uncertainty, alternatives, and recommendation conditions.
HUMAN GATE — Decide: an authorized person accepts, rejects, or revises the result when human approval is required.
RECORD — Preserve: retain the Decision Record and reopen it through History where persistence is available.

Trust through visible state

OMOS does not hide the difference between built and proven.

StateMeaning
ConceptualDefined idea; no operational implementation claim.
PrototypeDemonstrable implementation that is still incomplete or non-repeatable.
FunctionalAvailable for controlled validation with known limitations.
VerifiedAcceptance criteria and evidence have passed for the defined scope.
ProductionOperational, documented, repeatable, deployed, and supported with deployment evidence.
Verification rule: model agreement is not factual verification. A claim may still require external evidence, source review, professional judgment, or human authorization.
