Architecture Boundary

Agreement is not truth.

OMOS can compare convergence, contradiction, dissent and missing information. Factual verification remains a separate evidence function, and consequential decisions remain subject to human authorization.

Current maturity remains component-based. “Functional” means available for controlled validation with known limitations; it does not imply that every planned OMOS capability is Verified or Production.
