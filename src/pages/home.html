Why OMOS

AI gives answers. OMOS gives you a decision process.

Instead of asking one model and accepting one response, OMOS structures the question, identifies uncertainty and evidence needs, compares available model perspectives, preserves contradictions, and produces a governed result for human review.

01

Distill messy information

Separate objectives, known facts, assumptions, constraints, contradictions, missing information and risky instructions before synthesis begins.

02

Compare AI perspectives

Use OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini and xAI provider interfaces when configured, while clearly labeling simulation, hybrid, live and degraded participation.

03

Keep a reviewable record

Decision Records preserve versions, provenance, Council output, alignment state, human disposition and revision lineage where durable persistence is enabled.

OMOS integrity rule

Provenance precedes synthesis. Evidence outranks model agreement. Roles belong to the transaction—not permanently to a vendor. GCD-inspired synthesis can identify common ground, but it is not mathematical proof that a factual conclusion is true.
