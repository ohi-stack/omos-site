Alignment • Dimensions • Hard Gates

Evaluate decision quality across explicit dimensions, not one magical score.

The OMOS Alignment Engine produces structured decision-support signals after Distill. Current runtime logic separates dimensions such as truth support, clarity, coherence, dignity, constructive unity, evidence, verifiability, confidence, and execution readiness, while hard gates can require review or block progression.

Why dimensions matter

A single opaque score can hide important tradeoffs. OMOS should expose which dimensions are strong, which are weak, why a gate was triggered, and what evidence would change the state.

Hard gates

Configured gates can flag instruction-integrity problems, weak evidence, dignity/coercion risks, authorization conflicts, fabricated evidence, or other high-risk conditions. A blocked or review-required state should not be averaged away by a strong score elsewhere.

Status is not truth

Alignment scoring is a structured decision-support mechanism. It does not independently establish factual truth, legal validity, scientific proof, or professional suitability.
