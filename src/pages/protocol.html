The OneGodian Protocol™

The Protocol defines the terms, identity semantics, scope, interoperability rules, human-authority boundaries, and implementation guidance that OneGodian-aware systems use. It is distinct from the OneGodian Algorithm™ and from the OMOS runtime.

Open AlgorithmDocumentationRuntime Manifest

Definitions

Canonical terminology, meaning, scope, naming, and relationship rules for OneGodian systems and interfaces.

Identity Semantics

Structured ways to represent voluntary identity, role, context, declaration, and user-supplied meaning without treating software output as legal authority.

Interoperability

Provider-neutral contracts for models, applications, plugins, APIs, data sources, and execution systems.

Human Authority

Models, agents, services, and O-H-I processes provide analysis and execution support. They do not replace the human decision-maker or silently escalate authority.

Transparency

Operational systems should expose version, provenance, maturity, uncertainty, verification status, and whether outputs were simulated, degraded, or live.

Voluntary Use

The Protocol is a framework for documentation, software, identity reflection, interoperability, and education. It does not claim compulsory authority over non-participants.
