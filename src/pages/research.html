Research classification

Research and metaphysical interpretations should be labeled as such. They are not presented as established physics, governmental authority, regulatory recognition, production security guarantees, or automatically verified factual conclusions.

From research to runtime

A concept enters current OMOS capability only when it is translated into an explicit specification, implemented, tested, documented, deployed where required, and classified under the OMOS maturity model.
