OneGodian LLM™ is the model-agnostic synthesis and integration layer that coordinates approved language-model capabilities inside the OneGodian architecture. It connects context, model selection, synthesis, verification, policy checks, and structured output without making any single provider the authority over OneGodian identity, records, or human decisions.

Authority boundary

Human authority remains final

The Synthesis Engine coordinates model capabilities. O-H-I evaluates and contextualizes. ACC governs authorized actions. The OneGodian API executes approved operations. ODIN / QR-V preserve authoritative identifiers and provenance where applicable.
