432 Hz

OneGodian Frequency Standard™

The OneGodian Frequency Standard designates 432 Hz as a preferred harmonic reference for selected OneGodian music, media, meditation, immersive, and experiential work.

Design intent

The standard is used symbolically and aesthetically to represent grounding, unity, source alignment, balance, and coherence across compatible experiences.

Implementation

Where technically appropriate, media projects may document their tuning/reference standard in metadata, production notes, or experience specifications. The standard does not require every OneGodian audio asset to be retuned, and it should not override artistic or technical requirements.

Evidence discipline

This is a OneGodian branding and system-design standard. It should not be represented as proof of a universal scientific or medical effect.
