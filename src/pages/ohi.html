Agreement Zones

What multiple providers independently identify in common. Agreement is useful comparative evidence but does not establish factual verification.

Contradictions

Where models disagree, make incompatible assumptions, or produce mutually inconsistent recommendations.

Missing Evidence

Questions, sources, data, or context that remain unresolved and may materially change the recommendation.

Novel Insights

Potentially useful observations that appear in one or a minority of outputs and should not be discarded merely because they lack consensus.

Supported Dissent

Minority positions that remain plausible or evidence-supported after review. OMOS preserves them instead of averaging them away.

Governed Output

Conclusions, alternatives, uncertainty, evidence state, confidence, recommended path, conditions that would change the recommendation, and human-review requirements.

GCD-style synthesis

Reduction is a methodology, not automatic proof.

OMOS may use a GCD-style metaphor for reducing model-specific noise and finding a defensible shared foundation. In production reasoning, disagreements, edge cases, minority evidence, and unresolved contradictions remain visible. The system must not simply remove everything that is not shared by all models.

Verification rule: model agreement, simulation output, internal review, or mathematical elegance does not by itself establish an external fact, scientific discovery, legal conclusion, or institutional authority.
