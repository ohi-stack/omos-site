O-H-I • Multi-Model Review • Governed Synthesis

OHI compares intelligence outputs without flattening their differences.

Within OMOS, OHI — OneGodian Hyper-Conscious Intelligence — is the multi-model comparison, critique, synthesis, and human-reviewed intelligence layer. Its job is not to average AI answers. It structures overlap and divergence, surfaces contradictions, preserves meaningful dissent, identifies missing evidence, and prepares a governed synthesis for human review.

Council inputs

Configured providers can contribute independent analyses. Current OMOS provider architecture includes OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, and xAI, while future providers and OLLM should enter through the same role- and capability-oriented contract. Provider brands do not own fixed reasoning roles.

GCD Synthesis

OHI uses a GCD-style repeated-reduction method to expose common ground while retaining material remainders. The method is useful because disagreement can contain the risk, evidence gap, or alternative that matters most.

Verification boundary

Model agreement is not factual verification. OHI can tell you what multiple models share or dispute; verification asks whether those claims survive contact with external evidence.

Governed output

A governed synthesis should make conclusions, assumptions, dissent, evidence state, uncertainty, risks, alternatives, and human-review requirements explicit. The Human Gate determines the recorded disposition; external consequential execution requires separate authorization.

Provenance

OMOS must establish who actually produced each contribution before using cross-provider convergence as evidence. Runtime-captured provider/model/request metadata is stronger provenance than a manually relayed label.
