OneGodian Hyper-Conscious Intelligence™ · O-H-I

Compare intelligence without erasing disagreement.

Within OMOS, O-H-I is the comparison, critique and synthesis layer. It receives independent provider outputs, identifies common ground and material remainders, maps contradictions and missing evidence, and produces a governed synthesis for human review.

Core integrity rule: provenance precedes synthesis. Evidence outranks model agreement. A unanimous Council is still not independent factual verification.

Independent Outputs

Providers contribute independently before cross-review so early consensus pressure does not collapse distinct reasoning.

Cross-Model Review

Each eligible provider reviews other outputs for agreement, contradiction, missing evidence, risks and novel insight. Self-review is not treated as independent verification.

GCD-Inspired Synthesis

OMOS may use a repeated comparison/reduction method inspired by Greatest Common Divisor logic to find common ground. This is a synthesis metaphor and method, not mathematical proof that a factual conclusion is true.

Material Remainders

Provider-specific evidence, dissent or constraints that could change a decision must be preserved rather than discarded merely because they are not shared.

Verification State
