Verification • Evidence • Uncertainty

Agreement is not verification.

OMOS separates what models reason from what evidence establishes. Council convergence can reveal useful common ground, but a factual claim becomes stronger only when its source, evidence, scope, timing, and verification status are independently examined.

Evidence hierarchy

Evidence → Verified State → Reasoned Consensus → Confidence.

Confidence is a decision-support signal, not proof. A deterministic schema or state transition can be reproducible without making the underlying factual claim true.

Verification record

A verification result should identify the claim, supporting evidence, source references, contradictions, freshness or timestamp limits where relevant, verification method, status, and unresolved conditions. Missing evidence should remain explicit rather than being filled by model confidence.

Useful result states

OMOS can distinguish states such as SUPPORTED, PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED, INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE, CONFLICT_UNRESOLVED, and NOT_VERIFIED. The exact production schema is version-controlled and may evolve.

Human review

Verification informs the Human Gate; it does not replace it. Professional legal, financial, medical, tax, engineering, or other regulated judgments may require qualified external review beyond OMOS.

External verification infrastructure

QR-V, OBP-1, registries, source systems, and other OneGodian or third-party evidence services may be connected where separately implemented and authorized. Their existence elsewhere in the ecosystem does not imply that every OMOS run currently invokes them.
