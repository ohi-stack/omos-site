The 4×4 Cross-Evaluation Matrix

In a standard setup, querying four models produces four disconnected answers. In OMOS, each model is re-prompted with the other three anonymized answers. This cross-review exposes subtle flaws that no single prompt could detect:

Agreement Zones

Core claims and facts endorsed independently by all four providers, representing high-probability factual consensus.

Contradictions

Direct factual or logical conflicts identified between providers, flagged immediately for human review.

Missing Angles & Omissions

Crucial points identified by one model that were overlooked or omitted by others during initial generation.

Novel Insights

Unique lateral connections or creative problem-solving steps generated by an individual system that survive scrutiny.
