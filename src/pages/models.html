Provider-neutral contract

OMOS governs the workflow; providers remain replaceable.

Every Council contribution should carry provider, model, connector identity, request ID where available, timestamps, hashes, simulation state, and provenance status. Roles such as architect, builder, verifier, and adversarial reviewer belong to the transaction—not permanently to a vendor.

Verification boundary: connector health proves a provider path is configured or responding. It does not prove a model answer is true. External evidence and human authorization remain separate controls.
