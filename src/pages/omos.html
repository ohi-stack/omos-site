OMOS is the governed operating layer around complex AI-assisted decisions.

OMOS receives and distills human requests, evaluates alignment, coordinates multiple model providers, preserves disagreement and evidence gaps, produces governed synthesis, keeps human authority explicit, persists Decision Records, and connects approved systems through auditable interfaces.

Canonical governed transaction
1 — Ask OMOS: capture the decision, problem, document, or project context.
2 — Layer 1 / Distill: preserve objective, claims, requirements, evidence, ambiguity, contradictions, and quarantined instructions.
3 — Alignment: evaluate explicit dimensions and hard gates.
4 — Council: collect independent outputs from configured providers and perform cross-review.
5 — Governed Synthesis: preserve agreement, contradictions, missing evidence, novel insights, supported dissent, uncertainty, and recommendation conditions.
6 — Human Gate + Decision Record: accept/reject the governed result and persist auditable state.
7 — History: reopen, inspect, compare, replay where supported, and verify record lineage.
