OMOS • Architecture • Operational Intelligence

OMOS is the governed operating layer around complex AI-assisted decisions.

OMOS receives and distills human requests, evaluates alignment, coordinates multiple model providers, preserves disagreement and evidence gaps, produces governed synthesis, keeps human authority explicit, persists Decision Records, and connects approved systems through auditable interfaces.

Canonical governed transaction
1 — Ask OMOS: capture the decision, problem, document, or project context.
2 — Layer 1 / Distill: preserve objective, claims, requirements, evidence, ambiguity, contradictions, and quarantined instructions.
3 — Alignment: evaluate explicit dimensions and hard gates.
4 — Council: collect independent outputs from configured providers and perform cross-review.
5 — Governed Synthesis: preserve agreement, contradictions, missing evidence, novel insights, supported dissent, uncertainty, and recommendation conditions.
6 — Human Gate + Decision Record: accept/reject/revise the governed result and persist auditable state.
7 — History: reopen, inspect, compare, replay where supported, and verify record lineage.
Connection architecture

Model Gateway — who OMOS reasons with

OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, xAI, and future OLLM/provider interfaces sit behind a normalized connector contract. Live, Hybrid, Degraded, and Simulation states remain explicit.

Model Connectors →

Data Connector Gateway — what OMOS reads or acts upon

GitHub, Google Drive, WordPress, Stripe, PostgreSQL/Supabase, QRV, and approved OneGodian APIs retain source-of-record ownership, permissions, sync direction, IDs, timestamps, provenance, conflict policy, and audit events.

Connector Architecture →
Source-of-record rule: OMOS may correlate external records without pretending to originate them. A Stripe commercial record remains Stripe-sourced; a GitHub commit remains GitHub-sourced; a Drive document remains Drive-sourced; verified external state requires its own evidence and authorization.
