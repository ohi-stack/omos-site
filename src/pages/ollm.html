OLLM • Model Gateway • OneGodian Intelligence Layer

OneGodian LLM inside the OMOS model architecture.

OLLM is the OneGodian model and multi-model application track. Within OMOS it is treated as a first-class provider and governed intelligence surface, not as a replacement for the external foundation-model providers the Council can use.

Target journey

Discover → Account → Authenticate → Dashboard → Prompt → Select models → Execute → Compare → OHI governance → Synthesized output → Save → History

OMOS relationship

OMOS supplies the orchestration, alignment, provenance, persistence, Human Gate, and Decision Record controls around model execution. OLLM can participate through the Model Gateway alongside provider-neutral adapters. Individual provider outputs must remain distinguishable from the governed synthesis.

Production-track requirements

OLLM should not be represented as Production until authenticated access, real server-side model execution, persistent history, failure/retry handling, secret isolation, execution logs, user-facing errors, CI, operator documentation, and a working entitlement pathway are operational and repeatable.

Human authority

OLLM and external models provide reasoning and decision support. They do not acquire independent authority to approve consequential legal, financial, identity, registry, credential, infrastructure, or external execution.

Current status

This page documents the production-track architecture. Capability claims on the live site remain gated by current OMOS evidence.
