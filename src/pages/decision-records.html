Records • History • Human Disposition

Keep the reasoning, disagreement, evidence state, and human decision together.

An OMOS Decision Record is the durable record of a governed run. It is designed to preserve enough context to understand what was asked, how the runtime processed it, what providers contributed, what conflicted, what remained uncertain, and what the human ultimately approved or rejected.

Record contents

A consequential record can include input and output hashes, runtime/schema versions, timestamps, Layer 1 state, alignment dimensions and gates, requested and responding providers, simulation/live state, Council outputs, cross-review, contradictions, missing evidence, Governed Synthesis, verification state, Human Gate disposition, persistence metadata, ownership, revision number, record hash, and previous-record hash.

Human Gate

The Human Gate converts decision support into an explicit human disposition. Approval of the record does not automatically authorize every external legal, financial, credential, registry, infrastructure, or production action.

Revision discipline

Corrections and new evidence should create traceable revisions or superseding records where appropriate. OMOS should not silently rewrite an earlier record merely to make history appear cleaner.

Persistence

Production Decision Records require durable storage. OMOS-REF-0001 specifically requires the same governed Decision ID to survive a real runtime restart/redeploy and reopen with its integrity lineage intact.
