Council • Independent Reasoning • Structured Disagreement

Compare AI perspectives without pretending agreement is truth.

The OMOS Council is the multi-provider reasoning layer for difficult questions. It is designed to preserve independent outputs, compare claims, surface contradictions and missing evidence, preserve meaningful dissent, and prepare a governed result for human review.

What the Council does

For an eligible run, OMOS can request independent contributions from configured providers, preserve each response, perform cross-model review, identify agreement zones and conflict, then pass those findings into Governed Synthesis. A provider failure should produce an explicit degraded state rather than a fabricated consensus.

Roles belong to the transaction

OMOS may assign roles such as Architect, Builder, Adversarial Reviewer, and Verifier. Those roles are not permanently owned by OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, xAI, or any future provider. The role contract comes first; the provider is selected for that run.

Governance rules

  • No model is the authority of record.
  • No provider should serve as the sole final verifier of its own work.
  • Consensus is informative but non-authoritative.
  • Material dissent remains in the record.
  • Missing evidence remains missing.
  • Human authorization is required where the run reaches the Human Gate.

Council sequence

Same evidence → Independent reasoning → Role-aware review → Provenance → GCD extraction → Remainders → Contradictions → Evidence verification → Governed Synthesis → Human disposition → Decision Record
