ODIN-OMOS-0004 · Governance

Agent Authority Model

Defines the authority boundary for humans, OMOS, ACC, OCP, OEG, adapters, and agents. Intelligent systems may advise, classify, route, and prepare actions; consequential execution remains subject to explicit human or delegated authority.

Authority spine: Human Authority → OMOS Governed Reasoning → ACC Authorized Command → OCP Orchestration → OEG Execution Gateway → Adapter / Runner.

Approval records disposition. It does not convert an unsupported claim into fact.
