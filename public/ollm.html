OLLM — OneGodian LLM

OLLM is being developed as a OneGodian multi-model intelligence orchestration product and, within the OMOS architecture, as a first-class model/intelligence provider rather than a replacement for the OMOS runtime.

PROMPT → PROVIDER OUTPUTS → COMPARE → FILTER → NORMALIZE → GOVERNED OUTPUT → HISTORY / METERING

Application

Target application: llm.onegodian.org, with authentication, dashboard, provider selection and saved sessions.

Runtime

Provider abstraction, parallel execution, failure isolation, comparison/scoring, O-H-I governance, normalized results and audit logging.

Monetization

Free acquisition tier, professional subscription and business usage are development targets subject to actual metering and entitlement enforcement.

Status

Production upgrade in progress. OLLM should not be called v1.0 Production until authentication, live providers, governed synthesis, persistence, metering, paid path, monitoring and rollback are operational.

Human final authority is preserved. Raw model output does not become authoritative merely because a model generated it.
