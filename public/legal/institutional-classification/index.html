Institutional Classification

A clear operating boundary for describing OneGodian identity, ONEGODIAN, LLC commercial activity, and the separate Indigenous Nation of Onegodia context without blending their legal or organizational functions.

Core public classification

OneGodian is a founder-origin identity, philosophical, educational, and intellectual framework centered on unity and a singular divine source. People may hold OneGodian beliefs sincerely and religiously, while the commercial activities of ONEGODIAN, LLC remain distinct from the separate religious-society/community context of the Indigenous Nation of Onegodia.

ONEGODIAN, LLC

Use for software, intellectual property, publishing, education, products, commerce, consulting, licensing, and other private business activity.

Do not describe the LLC as a government, sovereign jurisdiction, church by default, or authority over nonmembers.

Indigenous Nation of Onegodia

Keep religious-society, community, internal-governance, membership, and related INO matters separate from the LLC's commercial operations.

Any statement about formal legal status, filings, rights, or authority should be tied to the actual governing documents and applicable law.

Banking and payment-processor language

For a ONEGODIAN, LLC account or commercial transaction, references to OneGodian should be framed according to the actual business activity: branding, authored intellectual property, software, education, publishing, products, services, or licensing. Avoid language that suggests the LLC is operating as a government or claiming an automatic religious tax classification.

Financial records

OMOS should preserve ordinary civil and financial controls. Gregorian dates control legal, banking, tax, contract, and invoice records; UTC is the canonical database timestamp; OneGodian Time may appear as a supplemental derived display under OTS-V5.

Claims OMOS must not make

  • That a copyright registration makes the word OneGodian legally exclusive in every context.
  • That an OMOS-generated declaration guarantees court, bank, government, employer, or agency recognition.
  • That OneGodian identity confers immunity from generally applicable law.
  • That ONEGODIAN, LLC possesses governmental or ecclesiastical authority.
  • That AI model agreement constitutes legal, scientific, or factual validation.

Operating rule

Describe the entity that is actually acting, the activity that is actually occurring, and the authority that actually applies. Keep authored framework, personal belief, religious-society context, and commercial software activity distinguishable in records and public communication.
