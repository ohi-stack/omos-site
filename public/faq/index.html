Frequently Asked Questions

What is OMOS?

The OneGodian Metaphysical Operating System is the governed reasoning, protocol, documentation, and runtime layer for the OneGodian ecosystem.

Does OMOS replace human decision-making?

No. OMOS structures evidence, model outputs, alignment signals, and Decision Records while preserving human authority for consequential actions.

Is model agreement verification?

No. Agreement is a signal; verification requires appropriate evidence and source checks.
