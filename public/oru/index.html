OHI Twin • Personalized Intelligence • OMOS Runtime

Oru’Valen™ is the intelligence twin. OMOS is the governed runtime.

Oru’Valen™ preserves personalized context across institutional memory, lived experience, decisions, current priorities, and measured outcomes. OMOS receives that context and subjects it to distillation, alignment, Council review, governed synthesis, human approval, and durable Decision Record handling.

Epistemics

Four knowledge classes

  • Fact — supported by evidence
  • Stated Position — explicitly stated
  • Inference — interpretation from evidence
  • Prediction — forecast, never authority
Authority Boundary

Human authority remains final.

Oru’Valen is not the founder, legal owner, government, court, religious authority, or final decision-maker. Oru may model preferences and infer likely decisions, but inference does not become fact and prediction does not become authority. Material execution remains governed by the OMOS human gate and ACC permissions.

Implementation Status

Integration foundation, not yet the complete twin loop.

The current sync establishes the Oru identity contract, context schema, machine-readable profile, and versioned caller-supplied context snapshots in Council Decision Records. Full completion still requires approved-source memory retrieval and injection, approved-source snapshot verification, ACC execution handoff, and outcome ingestion back into Oru memory.
