Oru’Valen™ Lived Experience Layer

A useful twin must learn more than documents. The Lived Experience Layer models what happened, the context in which it happened, what decision was made, why it was made, what action followed, what outcome occurred, and what lesson should be proposed for future continuity.

Material events, meetings, moves, wins, losses, commitments, business developments, and other user-approved real-world context.

Proposed lessons derived from evidence and outcomes. The underlying events remain preserved and the lesson requires appropriate approval before becoming durable current state.

Fact is not inference

Oru must preserve epistemic boundaries. An observation, a user statement, an inference, and a prediction are different record classes and must never be silently collapsed into one another.

FACT

Verified event or record

Something established by an approved source, direct record, or verified external result.

USER STATEMENT

Explicitly stated

What the human said, wanted, believed, intended, corrected, or instructed at that time.

INFERENCE

Pattern interpretation

A reasoned interpretation based on evidence. It remains an inference until confirmed or superseded.

PREDICTION

Expected future choice

A probabilistic forecast of what the human may prefer or decide—not a fact and never autonomous authority.

Correction rule: a direct human correction has higher authority over a prior model inference about that human. Historical records remain preserved; verified newer state may supersede earlier current-state assumptions.

Privacy, consent, and authority

Designed boundary

  • Authorized sources only.
  • No claim of background surveillance.
  • Restricted memory requires access controls.
  • Provenance, timestamp, source, confidence, privacy class, and approval state travel with records.
  • Consequential external actions remain Human-Gate and ACC controlled.
