Public and institutional boundaries

Entity separation

ONEGODIAN, LLC is the commercial, software, publishing, education and IP layer. INO governance/religious-society language remains separate and context-specific.

Provider neutrality

References to OpenAI, Anthropic, Google or xAI describe integrations; they do not imply endorsement or certification by those companies.

Time records

UTC is canonical for system timestamps. Gregorian remains controlling for civil, legal, banking, tax, contract and institutional use. OneGodian Time is derived supplemental display.

Professional scope

OMOS decision support does not replace qualified legal, medical, financial, tax or other regulated professional advice.
