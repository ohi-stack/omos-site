Agreement is not proof.

OMOS keeps several states separate so a polished synthesis cannot silently become a verified fact.

StateMeaning
Model agreementMultiple model outputs converge on a proposition.
Evidence supportIdentified sources or records support a claim to some degree.
Factual verificationThe claim has been checked against appropriate external or authoritative evidence.
AlignmentThe output satisfies the applicable OMOS decision-support dimensions and hard gates.
Human approvalAn authorized person accepts or rejects the governed result.
Registry statusA separate authoritative registry has actually recorded the item.
Deterministic execution can make a process repeatable without making the underlying conclusion true. Verification evidence should be attached and revisioned, not inferred from consensus.
